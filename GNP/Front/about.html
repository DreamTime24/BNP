About BNP

A journey of nationalism, democracy and leadership in Bangladesh

Our History

The Bangladesh Nationalist Party (BNP) was founded in 1978 by President Ziaur Rahman. It stands on the principles of democracy, nationalism, and economic progress for the people of Bangladesh. Over the decades, BNP has led the nation through key democratic transitions and remains a pivotal political force.

Our Vision

We aim to create a prosperous, independent, and sovereign Bangladesh where citizens enjoy freedom, justice, and equal opportunity under the rule of law. Our vision is rooted in cultural pride, economic empowerment, and people-centric governance.

Core Values

  • National Sovereignty
  • Democratic Rights
  • Social Justice
  • Economic Development
  • Transparency and Accountability

Major Achievements

  • Established multi-party democracy in Bangladesh
  • Implemented major economic reforms during governance periods
  • Expanded rural electrification and communication infrastructure
  • Increased global diplomatic presence for Bangladesh
  • Empowered local governments through policy decentralization

Founder’s Message

"Our goal is to build a self-reliant Bangladesh where democracy thrives and every citizen has the opportunity to prosper."

– Shaheed President Ziaur Rahman

Our Mission Today

Today, BNP remains committed to restoring democratic institutions, ensuring human rights, and rebuilding a Bangladesh where future generations can live in dignity, safety, and freedom.
